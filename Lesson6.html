STAGE 06 · UNDERSTANDING RISK

Before you trade...
understand the risk.

The stock market can create opportunities, but losses are always possible. Let's learn how to think about risk before putting money at stake.

◷ ~8 min • Beginner level
01
START HERE

There is no such thing as a risk-free stock.

Stock prices can rise and fall. A company can perform differently from expectations, markets can change, and unexpected events can move prices.

LOWER RISK HIGHER RISK
YOU

Different investments and situations carry different levels of risk. There is no universal "safe" level.

02
LOSSES

A loss is not just the number turning red.

If you buy a stock for ₹100 and its market price falls to ₹80, the investment is worth less than what you paid for it at that moment.

BOUGHT ₹100
↓
NOW ₹80
-20%
!

A price decline is a real risk. A stock does not have to return to your purchase price.

03
VOLATILITY

Some stocks move calmly. Others move like a roller coaster.

Volatility describes how much and how quickly a price can move. Higher volatility means larger price swings can happen over a period.

Lower movement

Smaller price swings.

Higher movement

Larger price swings.

04
DIVERSIFICATION

Don't put your entire basket on one egg.

Diversification means spreading investments across different assets, companies, industries or categories instead of depending entirely on one investment.

ONE BET
₹ ₹ ₹ ₹
One investment carries most of the exposure.
→
SPREAD OUT
₹ ◉ ◆ ₹
Exposure is spread across different areas.
!

Diversification can reduce the impact of one investment performing badly, but it cannot guarantee that you won't lose money.

05
POSITION SIZE

The size of a trade changes how much it can affect you.

Buying more shares means a price movement can have a larger effect on the amount of money involved. This is why experienced investors think about position size before entering a trade.

SMALLER POSITION
5
SHARES
₹100 × 5 = ₹500
LARGER POSITION
50
SHARES
₹100 × 50 = ₹5,000
💡

The example above is only showing exposure. It is not a recommended position size.

06
LEVERAGE

Borrowing can make both gains and losses bigger.

Some trading products allow investors to control larger positions using borrowed funds or margin. This can magnify outcomes — including losses.

WITHOUT LEVERAGE
₹1,000 exposure
×
WITH LEVERAGE
Larger exposure
⚠

Leverage is not free extra money. It can increase the size of both potential gains and potential losses. Beginners should understand the risks before using it.

07
THE HUMAN SIDE

Sometimes the biggest risk isn't the chart. It's the reaction to it.

Fear, greed, FOMO and panic can push people into decisions they didn't originally plan to make. A falling price can create panic, while a rapidly rising price can create FOMO.

😨
PANIC "It's falling! SELL!"
🔥
FOMO "Everyone is buying!"
💰
GREED "I'll keep going all in."
🧠
DISCIPLINE "What's my actual plan?"
08
SMARTER HABITS

Don't let a screenshot become your investment strategy.

Social media can make trading look effortless. But past gains, screenshots and promises of quick returns do not guarantee future results.

⚠
"Guaranteed returns"

Treat guaranteed-profit promises with extreme caution.

⚠
"Buy this NOW!"

Urgency is not research.

⚠
"Everyone is making money."

You rarely see the complete picture behind a screenshot.

⚠
Unregistered advisers or brokers

Deal only with authorised and SEBI-registered intermediaries.

BEFORE YOU TRADE

Ask yourself these questions.

01

Do I understand what I'm buying?

02

Do I understand how much money is involved?

03

What could happen if the price falls?

04

Am I relying on research or simply following someone?

05

Am I comfortable with the level of risk?

06

Am I using an authorised intermediary?

✦
KEY IDEA

Good trading isn't about eliminating risk. It's about understanding and managing it.

Risk and potential return go together. There is no guaranteed way to remove market risk.
