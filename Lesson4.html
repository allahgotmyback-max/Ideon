STAGE 04 · READING THE CHARTS

Okay... but
how do I read this chart?

Those red and green boxes aren't random. Let's break down what a stock chart is actually telling you.

◷ ~8 min • Beginner level
01
START HERE

A chart is simply a picture of price over time.

Instead of looking at thousands of individual prices, a chart puts them on a timeline so you can see how a stock's price has moved.

₹
₹80 ₹120 TIME →
02
TIME

The same stock can look completely different depending on the timeframe.

A chart might show one day, one week, one month, one year or much longer. Each timeframe tells you a different part of the story.

1D One day
1W One week
1M One month
1Y One year
💡

A price move that looks huge on a 1-day chart may look tiny when you zoom out to a 1-year chart.

03
CANDLESTICKS

This little box can tell you a lot.

One candlestick represents price movement during a specific period of time. It shows four important prices: open, high, low and close.

HIGH
LOW
Green candle

The close was higher than the open.

Red candle

The close was lower than the open.

04
OHLC

Four numbers explain one candle.

Every candlestick is built from four key prices. Together, they show what happened during that period.

O OPEN Where the period started
H HIGH Highest price reached
L LOW Lowest price reached
C CLOSE Where the period ended

05
THE WICKS

The thin lines tell you where price travelled.

The candle body shows the open and close. The thin lines, called wicks or shadows, show the higher and lower prices reached.

Long upper wick Price reached higher levels but moved back down.
Long lower wick Price dropped lower but recovered before the period ended.
06
VOLUME

Price tells you what happened. Volume tells you how much activity happened.

Trading volume shows how many shares were traded during a period. It can help you understand how active the market was around a price move.

07
TRENDS

Before looking for complicated patterns, look at the direction.

A stock can generally move upward, downward, or sideways for a period of time. This is the basic idea behind identifying a trend.

╱╲ ╱ ╱╲ ╱ ╱╲
Uptrend Price is generally moving higher.
╲ ╲╱ ╲ ╲╱ ╲
Downtrend Price is generally moving lower.

Sideways Price is moving within a range.
08
KEY LEVELS

Support and resistance are areas where price may react.

Think of support as an area where buying interest has previously helped price stop falling, and resistance as an area where selling pressure has previously helped stop price rising.

RESISTANCE
SUPPORT
💡

These are not guaranteed barriers. Price can break through support or resistance at any time.

✦
KEY IDEA

A chart helps you understand what price has done — not what it is guaranteed to do next.

Charts are tools for understanding market behaviour. They are not crystal balls.
