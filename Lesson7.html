BEFORE YOU CLICK BUY

A trade should start with a reason.

Before entering a trade, understand what you're buying, why you're buying it, how much you're putting at risk, and what could happen if the price moves against you.

In IDEON, we're going to practise this process without using real money.

03
CHOOSE YOUR QUANTITY

Quantity changes your exposure.

If one share costs ₹500, buying 5 shares means your position has a value of ₹2,500. Buying 50 shares would make the position ₹25,000.

The number of shares matters because a price move affects every share you hold.

5 SHARES ₹2,500 Example exposure
→
50 SHARES ₹25,000 10× the exposure
ⓘ

These are examples to explain exposure, not recommendations for how many shares you should buy.

06
UNDERSTAND P&L

P&L means Profit & Loss.

Your unrealised P&L shows how much your position is currently up or down compared with your purchase value.

If you eventually sell the position, the result becomes a realised gain or loss, before considering applicable charges and taxes.

07
SELLING

A trade isn't complete until you understand the exit.

Selling closes all or part of your position. The difference between your buying value and selling value determines the basic gain or loss before applicable costs and taxes.

BUY ₹500 per share
→
SELL ₹550 per share
BASIC DIFFERENCE +₹50 per share
ⓘ

Real trading can involve brokerage, taxes and other applicable charges. Your final result may therefore differ from the simple price difference.
