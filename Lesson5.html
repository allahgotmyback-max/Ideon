STAGE 05 · HOW ORDERS WORK

So... how do I
actually buy a stock?

You don't just press "buy" and magically own a share. Let's understand what happens when you place an order.

◷ ~8 min • Beginner level
01
START HERE

An order is simply your instruction to buy or sell.

When you use a trading platform, you're sending an instruction through your broker to the market. That instruction is called an order.

YOU Your decision "I want to buy 5 shares."
→
↗ Broker Sends your order to the market.
→
₹ Market Matches eligible buy and sell orders.
02
BUY OR SELL

Every trade has two sides.

A buyer wants to purchase shares. A seller wants to sell shares. The market brings these orders together when the conditions match.

↑
BUY

"I want to purchase."

You are looking to acquire shares of a company.

↓
SELL

"I want to sell."

You are looking to give up shares you already hold.

03
ORDER TYPE #1

Market order: "Get it at the best available price."

A market order tells the system that you want to buy or sell immediately at the best available market price at that time.

CURRENT MARKET ₹500
→
MARKET ORDER
BUY 2 SHARES At the best available price
💡

The exact execution price can differ from the price you saw a moment earlier, especially when prices are moving quickly.

04
ORDER TYPE #2

Limit order: "Only trade at my chosen price or better."

With a limit order, you specify the price you're willing to accept. The order can execute only if the market reaches a price that meets your limit.

CURRENT PRICE ₹500
₹500 CURRENT
₹480 YOUR LIMIT
BUY LIMIT @ ₹480 The order waits unless the required price becomes available.
Market order Prioritises execution at the best available price.
Limit order Prioritises your specified price, but may not execute.
05
ORDER TYPE #3

Stop-loss: an order that activates at a trigger level.

A stop-loss order remains inactive until the market reaches its trigger price. Once triggered, it enters the market according to the order type you've selected.

CURRENT ₹500
TRIGGER ₹460
01
You choose a trigger.

Example: ₹460

02
Price reaches the trigger.

The stop order activates.

03
The resulting order is sent.

Execution is subject to available market conditions.

⚠

A stop-loss does not guarantee that you'll exit at exactly the trigger price. Fast-moving markets can affect execution.

06
QUANTITY

Price tells you how much one share costs. Quantity tells you how many you want.

When placing an order, you normally specify the number of shares you want to buy or sell.

PRICE / SHARE ₹250
×

=
VALUE ₹1,000
💡

This simple calculation does not include applicable taxes, fees or other charges.

07
AFTER YOU PRESS BUY

Placing an order doesn't always mean it executed.

Your order can be pending, partially filled, fully executed or cancelled depending on the order conditions and market activity.

…
PENDING The order is waiting to be matched.
½
PARTIALLY FILLED Only part of your requested quantity has executed.
✓
EXECUTED The order has been successfully matched.
×
CANCELLED The order is no longer active.
