01
THE SIMPLE VERSION

A stock is simply a tiny piece of a company.

Imagine a company is worth ₹10 crore. Instead of one person owning the entire company, it can be divided into millions of tiny pieces.

Those pieces are called shares. When you buy a share, you own a very small part of that company.

COMPANY ₹10 CRORE

One tiny piece = one share

02
WHY DO PEOPLE BUY STOCKS?

Because companies can become more valuable.

Let's say you buy a share for ₹100.

The company performs well, more people want its shares, and the market price rises to ₹130.

YOU BUY ₹100
→
PRICE BECOMES ₹130

If you sell at ₹130, your gain before applicable charges and taxes would be ₹30.

03
TRADING VS INVESTING

They sound similar. They're not exactly the same.

Both involve buying and selling investments, but the main difference is usually the time horizon and approach.

⚡

Trading

Usually focuses on shorter-term price movements.

SHORTER TERM
🌱

Investing

Usually focuses on owning assets for the longer term.

LONGER TERM
04
THE IMPORTANT PART

Prices can go up. They can also go down.

The stock market does not guarantee profit. If the share you bought falls in price, your investment can lose value.

That's why understanding risk is just as important as understanding profit.

YOUR STOCK ₹100
↓
₹80 -20%
Profit is never guaranteed.
💡
REMEMBER THIS

Buying a stock means owning a tiny piece of a company.

Its market price can change based on what investors think that company is worth.
